Support

Questions, a bug, a rule that looks wrong? Write to duoentertainmentapps@gmail.com with your iPhone model and the game. We answer every message.

Playing on iPhone Duo

  • Flat on the table: unfold your iPhone Duo and play face to face on the big inner screen.
  • Two screens: half open it like a small laptop, open a two-player game, tap “…” and turn on “Two screens (camera)”. iOS lends the outer screen to an app only while its camera shows a live preview, so a tiny preview appears on your screen; nothing is recorded.
  • On any other iPhone, lay it flat between you. Games with secrets use “Pass the phone”.

Purchases

  • Two games in each app are free. “Unlock all games” is a single purchase, shared with your family.
  • On a new iPhone, open the app, tap a game with a padlock, then “Restore purchases”.

Privacy Policy

In short. DuoGames collects no data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Camera (iPhone Duo, optional)

Only if you turn on “Two screens (camera)” on iPhone Duo. The outer camera runs so that iOS lets the app use the outer screen; its picture is shown in a tiny preview on your screen. It is never recorded, stored or sent.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

Only if a player chooses to play by voice. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone to play the move; nothing is recorded or sent.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. DuoGames never sees your payment details.

What stays on the iPhone

Your settings (rules, language, two-screen choice) are stored on your iPhone only.

Children

DuoGames is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with the date.
